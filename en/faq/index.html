Custom websites

How much does a website cost?

We have no price list of packages; every website is priced to its brief, by scope, features and how much design it needs. After a short call you get a written offer with price and timeline. Half is paid at the start and half on handover.

How long until the website is ready?

It depends on the scope. A smaller site is usually ready in a few days to three weeks; an e-shop or an application takes longer. The exact deadline is part of the offer and starts once we have your materials.

Do you design as well, or only build?

Both. We design every website from a first sketch for your brand and show you the design before any code is written. If you already have a finished design, we can build the site from it.

Who owns the finished website and code?

You do. Once the full price is paid, the economic rights to the code, design and copy we created for you pass to you.

Will I be able to edit the website myself?

Yes, if you need to. We build sites so that people without a technical background can change everyday content, and we show you how at handover. If you would rather not, we look after it.

SEO

What is SEO, in one sentence?

A set of changes on your site and around it that make Google show you higher when people search for what you offer. You do not pay for clicks, you pay for the work that gets you there.

Who is SEO suitable for?

For any website that wants to acquire customers through Google, from local craftsmen and restaurants, through e-commerce stores, to startups and larger companies.

How long does SEO take to see results?

First measurable results typically appear within 60-90 days. Significant growth in organic traffic occurs after 4-6 months of regular work. SEO is a long-term strategy and the results remain even after the collaboration ends.

How much does SEO optimization cost?

Scope and price are agreed around what the site needs: sometimes a one-off audit and fixes are enough, sometimes ongoing work makes sense. You get the offer in writing up front, and the first audit is free and without obligation.

What is included in the free SEO audit?

The free audit includes technical website analysis, keyword overview, page speed evaluation, and summary of main improvement opportunities. You'll receive results within 3 business days via email.

Do I need to change my website content?

It depends on the audit results. In some cases, technical adjustments are enough, but we achieve the best results with a combination of technical SEO and quality content. We can also provide content creation.

Do I need a new website for this?

Usually not. In most cases changes to the existing site are enough. If a site were technically beyond saving we would say so in the audit, not six months into paying.

GEO

What is GEO and how does it differ from SEO?

SEO is about ranking high in Google's list of results. GEO is about being cited by an AI assistant when someone asks it a question and it replies with one answer instead of ten links. The goal is different: with SEO you want to be a result, with GEO you want to be a source the answer is built from.

Is GEO worth doing now, or is it too early?

It is worth doing, but not as a replacement for SEO. Most demand still comes from classic search, so we treat GEO as part of the same plan rather than an extra package. It also helps that most GEO work, meaning unambiguous facts, structured data and consistent company details, improves ordinary SEO too. So even if generative search slowed down, none of that work would be wasted.

How can GEO even be measured?

Not as reliably as SEO, and we won't pretend otherwise. There is no Search Console for AI answers. What can be done is asking the models the specific questions your customers ask, recording whether and how they mention you, then repeating it a month later and comparing. It's honest, but it's a sample rather than a statistic, and that is exactly how we will hand it to you.
