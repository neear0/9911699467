About us

Not a corporation.
A family business.

LumaWeb is one branch of AQUASHIELD s. r. o., a family business from Žarnovica, Slovakia. We build websites and handle SEO for companies that want to know who they are working with, and want to be able to reach that person.

One company, two branches

Who is behind LumaWeb

One family business runs two brands, each doing its own work. When a project grows from a website into custom software, it is handed over within the family, not to an outside firm.
