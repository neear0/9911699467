When people stop asking a search engine and ask an assistant

GEO is optimization for generative search: making sure an AI assistant names you when someone asks it about what you do.

An answer, not a top ten
Some people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. They do not get ten links, they get one answer with a few sources in it.
Being a source, not a result
It is no longer only about a position in a list. It is about whether the model picks you as a source it builds the answer from.
Content that can be quoted
Clear facts instead of marketing filler, structured data, the same company details across the web and mentions in the places models read.
We do not promise exact numbers
GEO is new and measuring it is not yet as reliable as with Google. We tell you what can be tracked and what is still an estimate.

What we actually do for GEO

There is no button for GEO. It is five concrete pieces of work, and after the last one it starts again from the first. Most of them help classic SEO too, so we do not sell it as a separate package but as part of the same plan.

  1. We find out what the models say about youWe ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity exactly the questions your customer would ask. We see whether they name you, who they name instead and whether they say anything wrong about you.
  2. We rewrite the facts so they can be quotedA model cannot quote "we are the market leader". It can quote a clear sentence with a figure it is able to check.
  3. We add structured dataWe mark in the code what is the company, what is a service, what is a price and what is a question with its answer. The machine then does not have to guess.
  4. We make the company details consistentThe same name, address, contact and description on the site, in Google Business Profile and in directories. When sources contradict each other, the model picks a company it is sure about.
  5. We measure againA month later we ask the same questions again and compare the answers. It is the most honest measurement GEO has so far, and that is exactly how we hand it to you.

What we find on sites most often

None of this is a disaster. But while it is there, it holds everything else back.

  • Pages Google does not seeThey exist and they open, but they are not in search.
  • Titles like "Home | Welcome"That is exactly what a person sees in Google. And exactly why they do not click.
  • Loading over five secondsLonger still on a phone. Many people leave before it is done.
  • The same description on every productTaken from the manufacturer. Ten other shops have the very same text.
  • Not a single link from outsideNobody links to you, so Google has no reason to believe you can be trusted.
  • A blog with three articles from years agoEither look after it or remove it. Like this it only does harm.

Most of this is fixed right in the code. That is why we build sites the way we do, so SEO does not start with repairs.
