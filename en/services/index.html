GEO

When people stop asking search engines
and start asking assistants

GEO is optimization for generative search, making sure AI assistants cite you when someone asks them about what you do.

What's happening

An answer, not a top ten

Some people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. They don't get ten links, they get one answer with a couple of sources in it.

What changes

Being a source, not a result

It's no longer only about a position in a list. It's about whether the model picks you as one of the sources it builds the answer from.

What we do

Content that can be quoted

Clear facts instead of marketing filler, structured data, consistent company details across the site, and mentions in the places models read.

What we don't promise

Exact numbers

GEO is new and measurement isn't as reliable as it is for Google. We'll tell you what can be tracked and what is still an estimate. Nobody serious can do better today.

Most of the work for GEO also helps classic SEO. That's why we don't sell it as a separate package, but as part of the same plan.

GEO in practice

What we actually do about it

There is no button for GEO. It's five concrete pieces of work, each with a reason why a model is more likely to pick you.

01
Find out what the models say about you

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity exactly the questions your customer would ask. We see whether they mention you, who they mention instead, and whether what they say is even right.

02
Rewrite the facts so they can be quoted

A model can't quote "we're a market leader". It can quote a specific sentence with a figure it can verify. Marketing filler simply doesn't get picked up in generative answers.

03
Add structured data

We mark up in code what is the company, what is a service, what is a price and what is a question with an answer. The machine then does not have to guess, it gets it readable, not as prose over a background image.

04
Make the company details consistent

The same name, address, contact and description on the site, in Google Business Profile and in directories. When sources contradict each other, a model prefers the company it's sure about.

05
Measure again

A month later we ask the same questions and compare the answers. This is the most honest measurement GEO currently allows, and that is exactly how we will present it to you.
