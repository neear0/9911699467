From the first talk to launch

After every step you know where the project stands. No months of silence followed by one big delivery at the end.

  1. Talk

    What the site should bring and who it is for. You know the price and the deadline in writing before we start.

  2. Structure

    Which pages the site will have and what goes on them. This is also where it is decided what Google will find later.

  3. Design

    We show you how the site will look before any code is written. Changes are cheap at this point.

  4. Build

    We build in code and you see the working version as it grows. Speed and mobile are handled from the start, not at the end.

  5. Launch

    Going live, redirects from the old site and a walkthrough. Then we look after the site, or you take it over.
