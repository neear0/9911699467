From sketch to launch

This is how every website we build comes together. Scroll on and watch the lines turn into a finished page.

  1. Sketch

    Content and order first: what a visitor should see first, where to go next and what to do in the end. No colour yet, so the substance gets decided.

  2. Grid and type

    The grid keeps order, the typeface gives the site its character. We choose both for your brand, and every page is then built on them.

  3. Colour and imagery

    Palette, photography and illustration that belong to your company, not to a template. You see the design before any code is written, so changes are cheap at this stage.

  4. Motion

    Transitions and details that lead attention where it should go. In moderation: the site should feel alive, not slow you down.

  5. Launch

    Fast code, mobile, analytics and technical SEO are done on launch day. Then you either take the site over, or we look after it.

What we build

Most briefs are one of these, or a combination. Scope and price are set by what the website has to achieve.

  • Web applicationsBookings, client areas and internal tools that run in the browser, with nothing to install.
