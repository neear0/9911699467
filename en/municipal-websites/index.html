What the rules require

Four things no municipal website can skip

These are not extras bolted on before an inspection. We plan for them from the first sketch, so the design holds together around them.

Official header

The header has to make clear at once that this is an official public administration website. Annex 12 of the decree sets out how.

Accessibility

The site has to work for people with poor sight, without a mouse or with a screen reader. The yardstick is WCAG 2.1 level AA.

Notice board and documents

Regulations, invitations and public notices with their publication dates, plus contracts, orders and invoices under Act No. 211/2000 Coll.

Search, RSS, English

Search on a larger site, an RSS feed for new content and the basic facts about the municipality in English as well.

Amendment 82/2026 Coll.

Changing the look of an older site?
The new wording applies

Amendment No. 82/2026 Coll. to the decree has been in force since 15 May 2026. For a website published by 14 May 2026, the new standards apply once its visual appearance changes substantially, in particular its typography, colours and header layout. We therefore build municipal redesigns to the new wording from the start.

Text of the amendment: Decree No. 82/2026 Coll. on Slov-Lex
