Void Solutions

Software and cybersecurityOur sister brand

Website of our sister brand for software development and cybersecurity. The whole home page sits on a 3D scene of points and lines that follows the pointer and opens into a spatial lattice as you scroll. Every service has its own page with its own 3D scene. The contact form runs on our own backend on Cloudflare, the site sends security headers (CSP, HSTS) and sets not a single cookie. In Slovak, English and German.

Z-izol

Construction company, roof waterproofing

Website for a company that has been waterproofing flat roofs and cladding halls in Slovakia and Germany since 2016. In the opening scene water runs across a roof into the drains, which is exactly what the company deals with. Then a gallery of completed projects, a page for every service, light and dark mode and three languages.

Metalcraft

Custom metalworks

Website for a metalworks with a tradition reaching back to 1994. The product range shows the parts as in a showroom: every mechanism is photographed closed and open, and one click switches between the two, one at a time or all at once. Then production capabilities, the workshop, their own product with a gallery and quote requests. A dark technical design with a grid like a technical drawing, in Slovak, English and German.
