What we build

Most projects are one of these, or a mix of two. We agree the scope around what the site actually has to deliver.

Company websites

A company site that says clearly what you do, who it helps and what the visitor should do next. You then change the texts and pictures yourself, no technician needed.

Often with several languages, content management and enquiry forms.

Online shops

Online selling with a structure both a customer and Google can find their way through. Categories, product pages and checkout are set up so they can grow without rebuilding the whole shop.

Catalogue and categories, payments and shipping, stock system links.

Web apps and portals

When the site should not only inform but do something: bookings, client areas, dashboards, internal tools. It runs in the browser, with no install, from anywhere.

Sign-in, bookings, overviews for you and for your customers.

Landing pages

One page built around one action: a campaign, a new product or an event. A short path from the first line to the button, with no detours to distract.

Campaign pages, variants to compare, measured results.

Public sector websites

Municipalities, towns, self-governing regions, public offices and the schools, libraries and other organisations they run. The site meets Decree No. 78/2020 Coll. and WCAG 2.1 AA and still does not look like another template.

What holds for every site

These are not promises for a proposal. They are decisions we make the same way on every project, small or large.

  • Designed from scratch

    No bought template. The look comes from your company, from what you sell and to whom.

  • Built in code

    No page builder you can never leave. The site is yours, code included, and anyone can take it over.

  • Fast on a phone too

    We work on speed and mobile during development, not once someone complains. A slow site loses people before the first line.

  • Ready for Google

    Page structure, titles, indexing and structured data are right on launch day. Which pages a site has decides what it can rank for at all.
