How it goes

From first call to launch

After every step you know where the project stands and what comes next. No months of silence followed by one big delivery.

01
Conversation and scope

We go through what the site has to deliver, who it's for and what you already have. That produces a scope and a price you know before any work starts.

02
Structure and content

Which pages the site will have, what's on each one and how you move between them. This is also where it's decided what the site can later rank for.

03
Design

We show what the site will look like before the code is written. Changes at this stage are cheap. After launch they aren't.

04
Development

We build directly in code. You see a working version as it goes, so nothing appears for the first time at the end. Speed and mobile are handled during the build, not after it.

05
Launch and care

Deployment, redirects from the old site, analytics and a handover session. After that you either take the site over or we look after it.
